History of Philosophy

Summarized & Visualized

This is an interactive visualization of the history of philosophy, showing the relationships — agreements and disagreements — between the ideas of major philosophers. Switch between Western and Eastern traditions to explore both intellectual lineages.

How to use:

  • Drag the canvas to navigate
  • Scroll to zoom in/out
  • Click a philosopher's name or idea to highlight related connections
  • Use the Filters panel to show only ideas in certain philosophical branches
  • Use the Index to jump to a philosopher
